Assistant Professor of Mathematics · Sultan Qaboos University

I am an Assistant Professor of Mathematics at Sultan Qaboos University. My research lies at the intersection of stochastic analysis, nonlinear evolution equations, and mathematical finance. In particular, I study stochastic and deterministic partial differential equations on Hilbert manifolds, asymptotic properties of stochastic systems, and the mathematical foundations of financial derivative pricing. My work also engages with uncertainty theory and computational approaches to applied mathematical problems.

WHAT I WORK ON

Research interests

Problems at the intersection of rigorous analysis, stochastic dynamics, and applications.

All research areas ↗
Stochastic analysis↗

SPDEs & geometric stochastic analysis

Well-posedness, regularity, qualitative behavior, and large-deviation principles for stochastic evolution equations, including dynamics constrained to infinite-dimensional Hilbert manifolds.

  • Nonlinear stochastic heat equations
  • Evolution on Hilbert manifolds and sphere constraints
  • Large deviations and asymptotic analysis
  • Stochastic Swift–Hohenberg-type equations
Analysis↗

Nonlinear evolution equations

Analytical methods for deterministic and stochastic partial differential equations, with emphasis on invariant structures, long-time dynamics, and functional-analytic techniques.

  • Semigroup and Galerkin methods
  • Constrained heat flows
  • Stability and dynamical properties
  • Higher-order and fractional equations
Financial mathematics↗

Financial derivatives & uncertainty

Mathematical models for pricing financial derivatives and credit/weather-linked contracts using stochastic processes, uncertainty theory, and operator-based techniques.

  • Credit and structural risk models
  • Weather derivatives
  • Exotic and path-dependent options
  • Liu processes and uncertain differential equations
Applied mathematics↗

Mathematical modeling & scientific computing

Selected collaborative applications of differential equations and computational methods to biological, environmental, and financial systems.

  • Physics-informed neural networks
  • Tumor–immune dynamics
  • Data-informed financial models
  • Numerical approximation of nonlinear systems
